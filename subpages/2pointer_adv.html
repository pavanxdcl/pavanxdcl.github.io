01 — FOUNDATION

What is Two Pointers?

Two pointers means maintaining two positions and moving them according to a proven rule.

←→

Two Indices

One pointer can start left and another right, or both can move forward.

⚡

Reduce Work

Instead of checking every pair, pointer movement can eliminate large parts of the search space.

🎯

Use the Constraint

Sorted order, boundaries or a monotonic condition usually tells you which pointer can move safely.

04 — COMMON PATTERNS

Three Two-Pointer Styles

Opposite Ends

Left starts at 0 and right at n−1. Common for sorted two-sum and palindrome problems.

Same Direction

Both pointers move forward. Common for filtering, partitioning and removing duplicates.

Fast + Slow

One pointer moves faster. Common in linked lists for cycles and middle-node problems.

Decision Rule

Pointer movement must reduce the search space while preserving correctness.
